# Child Safety Standards

**Last updated: July 14, 2026**

Renga ("we", "us") is committed to protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).
This page describes our published standards on this issue, consistent with app store child safety
requirements. It should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) and
[Terms of Service](terms.html).

## 1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

Renga has a **zero-tolerance policy** for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content or
conduct that sexualizes, endangers, or exploits minors, including but not limited to:

- Uploading, sharing, or linking to CSAM in any form (image, video, text, or AI-generated).
- Sexual solicitation, grooming, or sextortion of a minor.
- Content that sexualizes a minor, even where no explicit nudity is present.
- Using the App to facilitate or advertise trafficking or exploitation of a minor.
- Attempting to use the App to contact or befriend a minor for a sexual or exploitative purpose.

Accounts that violate this policy are permanently banned, and any content is removed, immediately
upon detection — the standard 24-hour report review window described in our
[Terms of Service](terms.html#7-reporting-moderation-and-the-strike-system) does not apply to CSAE;
such reports are treated as the highest priority and actioned as soon as possible.

## 2. Minimum Age and Enforcement

Renga requires all users to be at least **17 years old** to create an account (see
[Terms of Service, Section 1](terms.html#1-eligibility)). We do not knowingly permit minors to use
the App. Accounts believed to belong to a minor are removed.

## 3. Detection and Prevention

We use a combination of methods to detect and prevent CSAE on Renga:

- **Automated screening**: images, video thumbnails, and text posted to the App are automatically
  screened for policy-violating content, as described in our
  [Privacy Policy, Section 6](privacy.html#6-content-moderation-and-your-data).
- **User reporting**: any user can report a post, video, profile, or direct message directly in the
  App. See Section 4 below.
- **Human review**: flagged and reported content is reviewed by our moderation team.

## 4. How to Report CSAE

If you encounter suspected CSAE on Renga:

- **In-app**: use the **Report** action on the post, video, profile, or conversation in question and
  select the relevant category.
- **By email**: contact **[safety@renga-app.com](mailto:safety@renga-app.com)** with as much detail
  as possible (username/profile link, content link or screenshot, and description).

Reports are reviewed as a priority, and offending content and accounts are removed as soon as
possible upon confirmation.

## 5. Legal Reporting Obligations

Where required by applicable law, confirmed instances of CSAM are reported to the relevant national
authority (for example, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) in the United
States, via its CyberTipline) and law enforcement, and we cooperate with lawful requests from law
enforcement investigating CSAE.

## 6. Contact

Questions about this policy, or reports of suspected CSAE, can be sent to
**[safety@renga-app.com](mailto:safety@renga-app.com)**.
